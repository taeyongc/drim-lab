TECH TRANSFER 01

High-Speed Parallel Robot Design (0.5 kg Class)

LicenseeRobostar Co., Ltd.
Contract date2016.12.23
AmountKRW 55,000,000
Period2017.01.01 – 2026.12.31
TypeKnow-how

Technology

Optimal mechanism design and structural analysis methodology for a parallel robot capable of high-speed pick-and-place operation with a 0.5 kg payload. The package systematizes design know-how including motor placement, link length, and workspace optimization, suited to high-speed sorting and assembly lines for electronics, pharmaceuticals, and food.

Commercialization

Transferred to Robostar, a leading Korean industrial robot manufacturer, and applied to high-speed parallel robots for semiconductor and electronics assembly lines. This was the group’s first technology transfer contract and laid the foundation for a continuing series of commercialization successes.

TECH TRANSFER 02

Dual-Arm Robot Design and Fabrication (5 kg Class)

LicenseeRobostar Co., Ltd.
Contract date2017.02.08
AmountKRW 110,000,000
Period2017.01.01 – 2026.12.31
TypeIP + Know-how

Technology

Design and fabrication technology for a 5 kg-class dual-arm robot performing bimanual assembly, grasping, and transfer tasks. Includes DOF design of each arm, inter-arm collision avoidance and cooperative motion planning, standardized end-effector interfaces, and lightweight structural design.

Commercialization

Directly used in Robostar’s commercial dual-arm robot products, applied to automotive parts assembly, electronics inspection, and shipyard parts handling. Patents and know-how were transferred together under a long-term license through 2026.

TECH TRANSFER 03

Gravity-Compensated Manipulator Design

LicenseeSB Robotech Co., Ltd.
Contract date2017.12.22
AmountKRW 55,000,000
Period2017.12.28 – 2022.12.27
TypeIP

Technology

Manipulator design technology that passively compensates gravity torque using springs, counterweights, and mechanical linkages, reducing motor capacity and power consumption while improving motion precision — particularly effective for collaborative and service robot arms.

Commercialization

Transferred to SB Robotech and applied to its collaborative robot arm product line, realizing cost savings from reduced motor capacity and improved battery efficiency, strengthening the SME’s in-house robot development capability.

TECH TRANSFER 04

Dual-Arm Robot Operation and Motion Planning

LicenseeFRT Co., Ltd.
Contract date2018.09.30
AmountKRW 160,000,000
Period2018.08.01 – 2028.07.31
TypeKnow-how

Technology

Software and algorithm technology for autonomous task planning and execution by dual-arm robots: real-time collision-free motion planning, automatic task scheduling, sensor-feedback force/position control, and user teaching interfaces.

TECH TRANSFER 05

Absolute Positioning Accuracy Calibration for Robot Manipulators

LicenseeSmartMotion Co., Ltd.
Contract date2018.12.11
AmountKRW 55,000,000
Period2018.12.01 – 2023.11.30
TypeKnow-how

Technology

A methodology that identifies kinematic parameter errors from measured end-effector positions and applies them to a calibration model, improving absolute accuracy in real time — essential for CAD-to-part processes, offline programming, and high-precision laser machining.

Commercialization

Transferred to SmartMotion and embedded in high-precision laser machining and inspection systems, reducing absolute position error by tens of percent and contributing to quality competitiveness in aerospace, defense, and semiconductor manufacturing.

TECH TRANSFER 06

AmountKRW 50,985,972

TECH TRANSFER 07

Four-Finger Robot Hand (Industrial)

LicenseeYoungchang Robotech Co., Ltd.
Contract date2021.07.05
AmountKRW 67,000,000
Period2021.06.16 – 2031.06.15
TypeIP + Know-how

Technology

A multi-fingered robot hand inspired by the human four-finger structure. Multi-joint fingers with thumb opposition enable stable grasping of objects of diverse shapes and sizes — irregular parts, produce, and food — with a single hand.

TECH TRANSFER 10

Universal Vacuum-Suction and Vibration Gripper

LicenseePIE Robotics Co., Ltd.
Contract date2026.06.08
AmountKRW 140,800,000
Period2026.06.08 – 2032.06.07
TypeIP

Technology

A universal gripper integrating vacuum suction and vibration-assisted grasping in one unit. Vibration assists objects that suction alone cannot handle — porous or irregular surfaces — enabling stable grasping of heterogeneous items such as produce, food, and small parts without tool changes.

Commercialization

Transferred to PIE Robotics for agricultural and logistics gripper products under a six-year exclusive license through 2032. A flagship example of the complete research → transfer → commercialization cycle, born from the national agricultural robotics program (2022–2026).
